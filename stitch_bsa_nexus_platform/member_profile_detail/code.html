Biography

Sarah Jenkins is a recognized leader in proactive threat intelligence, specializing in state-sponsored cyber espionage attribution and mitigation. With over a decade of experience bridging the gap between technical analysis and strategic executive communication, she has successfully led intelligence operations that have thwarted multi-million dollar intellectual property theft campaigns.

Before joining CyberShield Dynamics, Sarah served as a Senior Intelligence Analyst for a global financial institution, where she developed automated indicator-of-compromise (IoC) sharing frameworks now adopted by several industry ISACs. She is a passionate advocate for open-source intelligence sharing and currently co-chairs the BSA's Cross-Sector Threat Committee.
